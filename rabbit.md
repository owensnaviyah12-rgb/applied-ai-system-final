# Rabbit care notes

General guidance only, not veterinary advice.

## Hay and water
Hay should make up most of a rabbit's diet and should always be available, along with fresh water that is refreshed daily.

## Fresh vegetables
Offer a measured portion of fresh leafy greens each day, and introduce new vegetables slowly.

## Cage cleaning
Spot-clean the cage daily and do a full clean on a regular schedule, since rabbits are sensitive to dirty bedding.

## Exercise
Rabbits need time out of the cage each day to run and explore in a safe, supervised space.
