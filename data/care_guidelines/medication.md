# Medication safety notes

General guidance only, not veterinary advice.

## Giving medication
Give medication at the same times each day, follow the label or your vet's instructions exactly, and ask your vet or pharmacist if anything is unclear.

## Missed medication
If a scheduled medication is missed, contact your vet for advice instead of guessing or doubling up.

## Storage
Store medication in its original container, out of reach of pets and children.

## Flea and tick treatments
Flea and tick treatments are species-specific, so never use a product made for one species on another without checking with your vet.
