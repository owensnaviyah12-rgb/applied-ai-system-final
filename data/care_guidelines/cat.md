# Cat care notes

General guidance only, not veterinary advice.

## Feeding
Cats do well with consistent meal times, such as breakfast and dinner, and fresh water in a clean bowl.

## Play
Short play sessions of 10 to 15 minutes once or twice a day help indoor cats burn energy and stay active.

## Litter box
Scoop the litter box at least once a day and keep it in a quiet spot away from food.

## Grooming
Brushing a few times a week reduces shedding and hairballs, especially for long-haired cats.
