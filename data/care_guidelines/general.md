# General pet care notes

General guidance only, not veterinary advice.

## Daily routine
Pets feel safer when feeding, walks and play happen at similar times each day.

## Grooming
Regular brushing keeps coats healthy and gives you a chance to spot lumps, skin problems or parasites early.

## Treats
Treats should be a small part of the daily diet, so use them sparingly for training or bedtime rewards.

## Water
Check that every pet has fresh water at the start and end of the day.
