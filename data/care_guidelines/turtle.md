# Turtle care notes

General guidance only, not veterinary advice.

## Feeding
Feed a diet suited to your turtle's species and remove uneaten food so the water stays clean.

## Tank cleaning
Turtles need clean water, so a good filter and regular partial water changes keep a tank healthier.

## Basking and light
Most pet turtles need a dry basking area with a heat lamp and UVB lighting. A vet or species guide can confirm the right setup.
