# Dog care notes

General guidance only, not veterinary advice.

## Daily exercise
Most adult dogs need at least one walk a day, and many need two or more. Needs vary with age, breed and health, so a vet can help set the right amount.

## Feeding
Dogs do best with regular meal times, such as breakfast and dinner. Fresh water should always be available, and hard exercise right after a big meal is best avoided.

## Training
Short training sessions of 5 to 15 minutes, repeated through the week, work better than one long session.

## Walks in hot weather
On hot days, walk early or late, bring water, and avoid hot pavement that can burn paws.
